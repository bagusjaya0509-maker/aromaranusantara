The product

Cloves for bulk sourcing

Our clove supply comes from Maluku and Sulawesi. Buyers use whole dried cloves in spice blends, food and beverage production, and essential oil processing. The specification should match the application: appearance, aroma, moisture and the proportion of stems or headless cloves all deserve attention before ordering.

Tell us your intended use and acceptable grade. If oil quality is part of your purchasing specification, include the test method and required result in your enquiry. Agree the sample and batch requirements before confirming the shipment.

Questions before ordering

Where are the cloves sourced?

Our catalog lists Maluku and Sulawesi as sourcing regions. Ask our team to confirm the origin and availability of the lot quoted for your order.

What is the minimum order?

The catalog minimum order is 1 metric tonne. Tell us your quantity and destination so we can discuss the shipment arrangement; a 1-tonne order is not the same as a full container.

Can I request a clove sample?

Yes. Contact our team to discuss a sample before placing a bulk order. Include your required grade, intended use and delivery destination.
