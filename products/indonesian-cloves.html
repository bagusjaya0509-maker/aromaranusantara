Wholesale buying

Compare Indonesian cloves quotations

A useful bulk price starts with a clear product specification. When comparing Indonesian cloves suppliers, ask each supplier to quote the same grade, net weight and delivery terms.

  • Define the physical grade. State the acceptable proportions of stems, headless cloves and broken buds, alongside your moisture and foreign-matter limits.
  • Identify the test you need. If your application requires an oil-content or eugenol result, specify the test method and request information for the lot being offered.
  • Compare the full quotation. Confirm the currency, loading port, destination, freight included, payment terms and price-validity date before comparing totals.

For packing calculations, 1 metric tonne of net product equals 40 bags of 25 kg or 20 bags of 50 kg. Confirm gross weight and any pallet requirement separately. These bag counts do not describe a full container load.

Questions before ordering

Where are the cloves sourced?

Our catalog lists Maluku and Sulawesi as sourcing regions. Ask our team to confirm the origin and availability of the lot quoted for your order.

What is the minimum order?

The catalog minimum order is 1 metric tonne. Tell us your quantity and destination so we can discuss the shipment arrangement; a 1-tonne order is not the same as a full container.

Can I request a clove sample?

Yes. Contact our team to discuss a sample before placing a bulk order. Include your required grade, intended use and delivery destination.

How do I get a current wholesale cloves price?

Send your quantity, required grade, bag size and destination port. Ask for a dated quotation stating the product specification and shipping costs included, so you can compare offers on the same basis.
