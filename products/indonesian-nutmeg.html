Wholesale buying

Compare Indonesian nutmeg quotations

Give each Indonesian nutmeg supplier the same purchasing brief. The product form, kernel specification and testing requirements should be clear before you compare a price per kilogram or tonne.

  • Specify the product form. Our catalog lists whole nutmeg. If you need a particular kernel size or mace as a separate item, state its quantity and requirements in the enquiry.
  • Describe the grade. Include size or count, permitted broken material, moisture and other acceptance criteria. Confirm what the quoted grade name means for the offered lot.
  • Identify the batch. Ask how the sample and any laboratory report correspond to the lot offered. Confirm the tests your importer requires before approving the order.
  • Match the commercial terms. Check net weight, bag size, currency, loading port, delivery terms and the quotation's validity date.

Keep a written record of the agreed specification and sample reference with the quotation. This gives your purchasing and receiving teams the same information when they review the shipment.

Questions before ordering

Which nutmeg grade should I request?

Tell us the application and the measurable specification your buyer needs. Our buyer guide explains ABCD and BWP grades; confirm the grade and lot availability with our team when requesting a quotation.

Can I order mace with nutmeg?

Our catalog includes mace availability by enquiry. Specify whether you need whole nutmeg, mace or both, with the quantity and packaging for each product.

What should I check before approving a shipment?

Agree the written specification and sample first. Ask about batch-specific test reports and confirm destination requirements with your importer or customs broker before shipment.

How can I request an Indonesian nutmeg wholesale price?

Send the product form, required grade or kernel size, quantity, packaging and destination port. Include any testing requirements so the team can discuss the appropriate lot and quotation.
