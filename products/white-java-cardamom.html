The product

Indonesian white cardamom for bulk orders

White Java cardamom, also known locally as kapulaga putih, is the round Indonesian cardamom listed in our catalog as Amomum compactum. Our sourcing regions are West and Central Java. Buyers use it in food seasoning, tea and beverages, bakery products and herbal preparations.

Confirm the botanical name when asking for a quotation. White Java cardamom and green cardamom are different products, so a green-cardamom specification should not be assumed to apply. Discuss pod size, colour, cleanliness, moisture and the sample you need before approving a lot.

Questions before ordering

Is white Java cardamom the same as green cardamom?

No. Our white Java cardamom is listed as Amomum compactum. Confirm this botanical name and approve a sample when your application normally uses another type of cardamom.

What colour and packaging are available?

Our catalog specifies white-to-cream pods in 25 kg or 50 kg PP bags. Contact our team about the available lot and any custom packaging requirement.

Can I request a sample before a bulk order?

Yes. Send your intended application, specification and delivery destination. Our team can discuss the sample and the details needed for a bulk quotation.
