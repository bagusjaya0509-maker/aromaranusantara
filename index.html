About Us

Your Trusted Indonesian Spice Export Partner

Aromara Trader Nusantara is an Indonesian spice export company dedicated to supplying premium-quality cloves, nutmeg, and White Java cardamom to international markets.

Working closely with local farmers and producer networks across Indonesia, we ensure every product meets strict quality standards for aroma, moisture content, cleanliness, and consistency.

Our mission is to connect the richness of Indonesia's spice heritage with global industries including food processing, seasoning manufacturers, herbal products, essential oils, and distributors worldwide.

  • Direct partnership with local Indonesian farmers
  • Strict QC — aroma, moisture, size uniformity
  • Professional export documentation & packaging
  • Long-term supply contracts available
  • Based in Bali, serving global markets
